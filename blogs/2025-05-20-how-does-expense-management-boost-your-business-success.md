The Saudi market experiences multiple peak shopping seasons throughout the year, including Founding Day , Ramadan , and Eid Al-Fitr . For e-commerce businesses, these periods present both opportunities and challenges. Effective cost and expense management is essential to maximizing these opportunities while maintaining profitability.
